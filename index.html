Free billing software for your shop

Counter Billing makes GST bills, keeps your stock and khata, and shows your sales. On your Windows PC, without internet.

Download for Windows

Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit. Nothing else to install.

Everything a shop counter needs, free

  • GST bills in seconds: scan a barcode or type the item, then print.
  • Your own bill design: choose the paper size and what prints on it.
  • Stock that goes down with every sale and up with every purchase, with low-stock alerts.
  • Barcode labels printed for your items.
  • Customer credit (khata): see who owes what, and record payments.
  • Cash, UPI and card payments, and a day close that adds them up.
  • Sales reports, including an HSN-wise tax summary.
  • Staff accounts and automatic daily backups.

No monthly fee and no sign-up. Your data stays on your PC.

Versions

Use the latest version. The one before it is here in case you need to go back to it: restore the backup you made before updating, because the newer version changes your data in a way the older one can't read.

    Install

    1. Download CounterBilling.exe and keep it anywhere, such as the Desktop.
    2. Double-click it. If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC", click More info, then Run anyway.
    3. Choose Use this PC, then create your admin account and enter your shop details.

    Update to a new version

    1. Open the Backup page in the app and click Back up now.
    2. Close Counter Billing.
    3. Download the new CounterBilling.exe and put it in place of the old one.

    Your bills and stock are kept separately from the program, so they stay as they are.
